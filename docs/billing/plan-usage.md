# Plan & Usage

The **Plan & Usage** page shows your current subscription plan and credit consumption. Navigate to it from **Profile → Billing → Plan & Usage**.

---

## Subscription overview

At the top of the page you will see your organization name, active plan badge (e.g. **PRO_PLAN (MONTHLY)** or **FREE TRIAL**), renewal or trial end date, and the total credits included with your plan.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization name** | The organization tied to your subscription. |
| **Plan badge** | Your active plan type and billing cycle (e.g. *PRO_PLAN (MONTHLY)*, *FREE TRIAL*). |
| **Renewal / Trial end date** | The date your plan renews and included credits reset, or the date your free trial expires. |
| **Credits included** | Total credits bundled with your plan each billing cycle. |
| **Manage Plan** | Click the **Manage Plan** button to view available plans, upgrade, or change your subscription. This will take you to the plans page. |

---

## Included credits

The **Included credits** section displays a progress bar showing how many of your plan's bundled credits have been consumed during the current billing period.

- **Used** — credits consumed so far (shown on the left).
- **Remaining** — credits still available (shown on the right).
- **Reset date** — the date the counter resets, which matches your plan renewal date.

:::note
Unused included credits do **not** roll over to the next billing cycle. They reset on your renewal date.
:::

---

## Extra credits

Below the included credits you will find the **Extra credits** section. Extra credits are purchased separately and do **not** reset on renewal — they remain available until fully consumed.

- **Available** — the number of extra credits you currently have.
- **Add Credits** — click the **Add Credits** button to purchase additional credits. Extra credits are available after the free trial ends.

---

## Free trial

If you are on a free trial, a banner at the top of the page will indicate the remaining days in your trial. During the trial:

- You receive a limited number of included credits (e.g. 100 credits).
- Extra credits are not available until the trial ends.
- Click **Manage Plan** or the **UPGRADE NOW** button in the banner to select a paid plan before your trial expires.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**What happens when I run out of included credits?**
Test executions will consume your extra credits if available. If both included and extra credits are exhausted, new runs cannot be started until credits are replenished.

**When do included credits reset?**
Included credits reset automatically on your plan renewal date each billing cycle.

**Do extra credits expire?**
No. Extra credits remain available until they are fully consumed, regardless of your billing cycle.

**Where can I see a detailed breakdown of credit usage?**
Head over to [Usage History](./usage-history) for a transaction-level log of every credit charge.
