# Organization

The Organization page stores your company-level details in SuperQA. This information helps identify your workspace and may be used for billing, support, and account management purposes.

Navigate to **Account > Organization** to view or update your organization profile.

---

## Organization Details

The page displays your organization's current information in a read-only view:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization Name** | The name of your company or team as registered in SuperQA. |
| **Industry** | The industry sector your organization operates in (e.g., Financial Services, Technology, Healthcare). |
| **Company Size** | The approximate headcount of your organization (e.g., 51–200). |
| **Created** | The date this SuperQA organization account was created. |

---

## Editing Your Organization Details

To update any of these fields:

1. Click the **Edit** button in the top-right corner of the page.
2. Modify the fields you want to update.
3. Save your changes.

> **Note:** Organization Name, Industry, and Company Size are editable. The **Created** date is set automatically when your account is first registered and cannot be changed.
