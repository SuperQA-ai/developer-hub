# Security

The Security page lets you update your account password. Keeping a strong, unique password is one of the most effective ways to protect your workspace and prevent unauthorized access.

Navigate to **Account > Security** to manage your password settings.

---

## Changing Your Password

To update your password, fill in the following three fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Current Password** | Your existing password. This is required to confirm your identity before making any changes. |
| **New Password** | The password you want to switch to. Must meet the strength requirements listed below. |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter your new password exactly to confirm there are no typos. |

Each field has a **show/hide toggle** (👁 icon) on the right side so you can reveal what you're typing if needed.

### Password Requirements

As you type your new password, SuperQA checks it against the following rules in real time. Each rule shows a ✓ (green) when met, or ✗ (red) when not yet satisfied:

- **At least 8 characters**
- **One number or symbol**

Both requirements must be satisfied before the **Update Password** button becomes active.

---

## Saving Your Changes

Once all fields are filled and the password requirements are met:

1. Click **Update Password** to save your new password.
2. Click **Cancel** to discard any changes and keep your existing password.

> **Tip:** Use a password manager to generate and store a strong, unique password for your SuperQA account. Avoid reusing passwords across services.
