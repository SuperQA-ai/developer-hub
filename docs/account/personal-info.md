# Personal Info

The Personal Info page (also called your **Profile**) is where you manage your identity within SuperQA. This information is associated with your account and may appear in team views, reports, and notifications sent on your behalf.

Navigate to **Account > Personal Info** to view or update your personal details.

---

## Your Profile Overview

At the top of the page, you'll see your **avatar** (showing your initials), your **display name**, and your **email address** — giving you a quick snapshot of how your account appears to others.

---

## Profile Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **First Name** | Your given name as it appears in the workspace. |
| **Last Name** | Your family name. |
| **Job Title** | Your role or position (e.g., QA Engineer, Product Manager). This field is optional and shows *Not provided* if left blank. |
| **Email Address** | The email address linked to your SuperQA account. Displays a ✓ **Verified** badge once your email has been confirmed. |
| **Member Since** | The date you joined this SuperQA workspace. This is a **Read-only** field set automatically at registration. |

---

## Editing Your Profile

To update your personal details:

1. Click the **Edit** button in the top-right corner.
2. Update the fields you want to change (First Name, Last Name, or Job Title).
3. Save your changes.

> **Note:** Your **Email Address** and **Member Since** date cannot be changed from this page. Contact support if you need to update your registered email.

---

## Logging Out

To sign out of SuperQA, scroll to the bottom of the sidebar and click **→ Log out**. You will be returned to the login screen.
