# Reports & Analytics

Welcome to the **Reports & Analytics** hub. Here you can monitor the health and stability of your applications using data aggregated from your test and release runs.

## Available Reports

- **[Release Confidence Score](./release-confidence-score.md)**: A launch readiness score based on manual Release Runs (Execute Now) for a specific build.
- **[Continuous Quality Monitoring](./continuous-quality-monitoring.md)**: A long-term trend analysis based on scheduled, automated Test Runs across your suites.
