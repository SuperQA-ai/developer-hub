# Team Management

The **Team Management** page is where you invite colleagues to your SuperQA workspace and oversee who has access. Every member of your team shares the same projects, test suites, and reports, so adding the right people is the first step to collaborative QA.

Navigate to **Configure > Team** to manage your team.

---

## The Team Member List

The main view shows a table of everyone currently in your organization:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The member's name and avatar initials. Their **role badge** is shown below their name (e.g., *Super Admin*). |
| **Email** | The email address associated with their account. |
| **Joined** | The date they accepted the invitation and joined the workspace. |
| **Status** | Whether the member is currently **Active** in the workspace. |
| **Actions** | Options to manage or remove the member. |

Use the **Search members...** bar to quickly find a specific person by name or email in larger teams.

---

## Inviting a New Team Member

To add someone to your workspace:

1. Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner.
2. In the **Invite Team Member** dialog, enter the person's **Email Address**.
3. Click **Send Invitation**.

The invited person will receive an email with a link to join your SuperQA organization. Once they accept and sign up, they will appear in the member list with an **Active** status.

> **Note:** You can only invite one member at a time. Repeat the process for each person you want to add.

---

## Roles

SuperQA uses role-based access within a workspace. The **Super Admin** role (shown with a pink badge) has full access to all settings, projects, and team management. Additional roles and permission levels may be configured based on your plan.
